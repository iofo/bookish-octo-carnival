Federal tax is an estimate only: single-filer, 2026 IRS brackets and standard deduction ($16,100), with the deduction and bracket thresholds assumed to rise with inflation while the rates themselves stay fixed. It ignores state/local tax, filing status, credits, and other deductions — not tax advice. Pre-tax (Traditional) reduces that year's taxable income by the savings amount before computing tax, matching a traditional 401(k)/IRA. After-tax (Roth) taxes the full salary and contributes what's left after tax, matching a Roth account. Net take-home also subtracts an estimated 2026 FICA tax (6.2% Social Security up to the $184,500 wage base, plus 1.45–2.35% Medicare), applied to gross wages regardless of the pre-tax/Roth toggle — this affects only the net take-home figure, not the Federal tax column or any withdrawal-year calculations.
